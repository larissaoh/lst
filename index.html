Questions

A few things worth knowing.

What's the difference between the tiers?

The free analysis flags three to five things worth a closer look, with a short written note explaining why. The Expert Review goes deeper: a full audit with prioritized findings, rewritten examples, and a follow-up call. Review + Implementation includes all of that, plus the improvements handled for you.

Is this automated?

No, never. Every listing is reviewed individually, against the same hospitality and product-design framework, by a real professional. Technology may support organization or admin, but the judgment, findings, and final quality review are always human-led.

How long does it take?

The free analysis usually comes back within 48 hours. Paid reviews have a turnaround confirmed before the project begins, kept tight since every review is scoped to a single property.

What should stay outside a public guide?

Keep door, gate, lockbox, garage, alarm, and other reservation-specific access credentials in a private guest message or booking-platform check-in flow.
