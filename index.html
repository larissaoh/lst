FAQs

What's the difference between the tiers?

The free analysis flags three to five things worth a closer look, with a short written note explaining why. The Expert Review goes deeper: a full audit with prioritized findings, rewritten examples, and a follow-up call.

Is this automated?

No, never. Every listing is reviewed individually, against the same hospitality and product-design framework, by a real professional. Technology may support organization or admin, but the judgment, findings, and final quality review are always human-led.

How long does it take?

The free analysis usually comes back within 48 hours. Paid reviews have a turnaround confirmed before the project begins, kept tight since every review is scoped to a single property.

Do you need access to my Airbnb account?

No. We only need the public link to your listing. We never ask for or need login access to your account, at any tier.
