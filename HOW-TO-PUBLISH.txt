HOW TO PUBLISH YOUR GUEST GUIDE WITH NETLIFY

Your downloaded package contains:

PUBLISH-THIS-FOLDER
Upload only this folder to Netlify.

KEEP-FOR-EDITS
Keep this folder private. It lets the builder restore the guide and verify included updates.

FIRST-TIME PUBLISHING

1. Unzip the downloaded package.
2. Open https://app.netlify.com/drop and sign in.
3. Drag PUBLISH-THIS-FOLDER into Netlify.
4. Wait for the live netlify.app link.
5. Open the link on your phone and test it before sharing.

Do not upload the outer package or KEEP-FOR-EDITS.

UPDATING LATER

1. Return to the Guest Guide Builder.
2. Choose Open and select the original package ZIP.
3. Edit and preview the restored guide.
4. Create the updated package.
5. Open the same Netlify project and deploy the new PUBLISH-THIS-FOLDER.

Every $5 purchase includes the first download and one updated download within 30 days. Older guides can still be imported and updated for $5 without starting over.

SAFETY

Anyone with the public link can view the guide. Never include door, gate, keypad, lockbox, garage, alarm, or other access credentials.
